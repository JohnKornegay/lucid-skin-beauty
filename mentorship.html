Who This Is For

Built for Estheticians
Who Want More

Many licensed estheticians graduate with the fundamentals, but the gap between school and a thriving professional practice is wide. The Lucid Mentorship Program was created to close that gap with real, hands-on guidance from a Master Esthetician with 15+ years of field experience.

This isn’t a course or a workshop. It’s a customized, 1-on-1 learning relationship designed around your specific gaps, goals, and growth areas.

This Program Is For You If...

  • You’re a licensed esthetician with gaps in your clinical training
  • You’re a student approaching graduation who wants a head start
  • You want mentorship from someone who has built a real business
  • You value practical knowledge over theoretical instruction
  • You’re committed to building a successful, sustainable esthetics career
