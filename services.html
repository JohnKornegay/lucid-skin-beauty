Level 01

Foundation Treatments

Our Foundation Treatments are designed for clients who want clean, healthy, consistently maintained skin. Whether you’re brand new to professional skincare, recovering from seasonal changes, or simply looking for monthly upkeep. This is your entry point to intentional skin health.

These treatments focus on deep cleansing, hydration, and restoring your skin’s natural balance. No harsh protocols, no downtime. Just results you’ll feel immediately.

Ideal For

  • First-time professional skincare clients
  • Seasonal skin refreshes (quarterly or monthly)
  • Maintenance between advanced treatment series
  • Sensitive skin needing gentle, consistent care
Book a Foundation Treatment
Level 02

Advanced Treatments

When your skin has specific concerns that need targeted clinical attention, Advanced Treatments are the answer. Using professional-grade equipment and evidence-backed product formulations, we address the root causes, not just surface symptoms.

These protocols are tailored by Santana based on a thorough skin assessment. Each session is designed to move the needle on measurable skin improvements under professional supervision.

Ideal For

  • Active acne and post-acne scarring
  • Hyperpigmentation and uneven skin tone
  • Rough texture and clogged pores
  • Compromised or impaired skin barrier
Book an Advanced Treatment
Level 03

Premium Programs

For clients ready to commit to a full skin transformation, Premium Programs deliver the most comprehensive results available at Lucid Skin. These are multi-session protocols that combine in-boutique treatments with a strategic at-home regimen, creating a consistent, layered approach to long-term skin health.

Premium Programs include a detailed consultation, a customized home skincare plan using our trusted product partners, and regular progress assessments throughout your program.

What’s Included

  • In-depth skin consultation and analysis
  • Multiple in-boutique treatment sessions
  • Customized at-home skincare regimen
  • Progress check-ins and plan adjustments
  • Professional product recommendations from trusted lines
